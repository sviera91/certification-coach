Your practice stays yours.

Lite stores progress in this browser, separately from the desktop canvas. It does not upload answers, scores, or flags. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove progress. The canvas stores progress on your computer; requested AI coaching goes through Copilot.

No analytics or tracking scripts are added by this project. GitHub Pages processes normal request metadata; external study links have their own policies. Public issue reports are yours to review and submit.
